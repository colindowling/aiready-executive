## The 30 days

A structured, time-bounded engagement. No open-ended retainers. No deliverable drift.

- **Days 1–3 — Intake and diagnostic.** Deep-dive interview with you and up to two key leadership stakeholders. The work maps your current AI posture, existing initiatives, board expectations, and where the real gaps live.
- **Days 4–10 — Readiness assessment.** Structured evaluation across five dimensions: leadership alignment, data and infrastructure, culture and workforce readiness, governance, and use-case pipeline. Written findings delivered by day 10.
- **Days 11–21 — Strategy and framework build.** Outcomes-first AI strategy built around your business, not a template. Tool evaluation framework, vendor criteria, and cultural change architecture designed for your org structure.
- **Days 22–30 — Blueprint and board narrative.** Final 90-day execution blueprint with sequenced milestones, owners, and decision gates. Board-ready AI narrative you can present with confidence. Live working session to walk through everything.

The day-30 outcome: a complete, defensible AI readiness strategy. Written assessment of your current state, an outcomes-first roadmap, a tool evaluation framework, a culture change architecture, and a board narrative — delivered in 30 days, built around your business, owned by you permanently.

## What you own at the end

Five concrete deliverables, all owned by you permanently:

- A written AI Readiness Assessment documenting your current state across five dimensions
- An outcomes-first AI strategy aligned to your specific business priorities
- A tool and vendor evaluation framework you can use independently going forward
- A 90-day execution blueprint with sequenced milestones, owners, and decision gates
- A board-ready AI narrative you can present with confidence

The six parts of the framework are the Executive AI Readiness Assessment, Outcomes-First Strategy Design, AI-Ready Culture and Change Architecture, the Tool and Vendor Evaluation Framework, the Board-Level Readiness Narrative, and the 90-Day Execution Blueprint. They are collected on the [advisory page](/advisory/#framework).

For the Solo Executive engagement, expect roughly 4–5 hours of your time across the full 30 days: the initial intake session, one or two working sessions during the strategy build, and the final delivery walkthrough.
