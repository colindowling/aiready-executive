## What the session page says you leave with

The [15-minute roadmap session](/15minutesairoadmap/) is separate from the paid engagement. The page lists five things you walk away with:

- Three quick wins: three places AI produces a visible result in your business inside 30 days, in an order specific to your operation.
- The five questions that put you back in charge of the room, for your CTO, your vendors, and your directors.
- A language pack: the terms your team will use, what each one means in plain English, and which ones the page calls vendor noise.
- A 90-day roadmap, sequenced so the first win arrives before the first objection.
- A resistance map: who will push back, what they are worried about, and what to say.

Work with us, don't work with us — the quick wins and the roadmap are yours to keep.

## What it is not

The session page says fifteen minutes cannot fix the organization. The readiness assessment, the outcomes framework, the vendor criteria, and the board narrative are the thirty-day engagement on the [advisory page](/advisory/). The call tells you whether you need one, including when the answer is no.

The same page says none of that paid engagement is a condition of the free session, and that engagements start at $20,000. The published fees are [Solo Executive at $20,000](/advisory/#solo-executive), [Leadership Trio at $45,000](/advisory/#leadership-trio), and [Full C-Suite at $75,000](/advisory/#full-c-suite).

## How the page says the 15 minutes go

No pitch. No deck. No flattery. The agenda on the session page is an introduction, then your quick wins and roadmap, then the five questions and how to use them.
