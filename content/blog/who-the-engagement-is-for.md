## The four seats the site names

This is not executive education. It is working advisory for leaders who have been told — or already know — that their AI posture is a strategic liability. The homepage names four:

- **CEO / President.** AI is now your mandate, not your CTO's. Own the strategy before the board demands an accounting.
- **Board member.** Ask the right questions. Evaluate management's AI claims against a framework, not instinct.
- **COO.** Your operations are the implementation surface. Build the culture and change architecture that makes adoption real.
- **CFO / Strategy lead.** Approve AI investments with a framework for outcomes, not vendor enthusiasm. Govern what gets funded.

## Above the Chief AI Officer layer

The site describes the engagement as executive alignment above the Chief AI Officer layer. A Chief AI Officer or Head of Innovation is responsible for execution. The question the FAQ answers is whether the C-suite and board are asking the right questions, and whether that leader is executing against a framework or a mandate with no guardrails.

The same FAQ says you work directly with a senior practitioner from intake to delivery. That practitioner is [Colin Dowling](/about/).
