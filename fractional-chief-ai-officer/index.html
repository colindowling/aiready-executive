Comparison

Fractional chief AI officer, advisor, or a consulting firm.

These are not three prices for the same thing. A firm sells the company a strategy. A fractional chief AI officer joins the operating rhythm. An open-ended advisor stays on call. A 30-day primer is for you, privately, and it ends.

If there is no chief AI officer and no internal AI team, you are the person the board will ask. The work has to leave you able to answer.

What each one is for

PathYou are buyingWhat you still cannot do
Large consulting firm A strategy for the organization, usually produced by a team you will not keep. Judge the next proposal yourself. The deck does not teach you that.
Fractional chief AI officer Part of a leadership seat. Useful when someone must run the work week to week. Skip the seat. If the open question is your own judgment, a title next to you does not answer it.
Open-ended advisor Access. A retainer with no end date and no single artifact. Know when you are done. The relationship can outlast the decision.
30-day primer One executive, in private, for a month. The output is a written position for this business. Hand the building to someone else and stay out of it. Leading it is the point. Scope is still a draft: what the 30 days covers.

University programs and self-paced courses sit further away. They take longer, they put you in a group, and they end with a certificate rather than a position you can defend in your own board meeting.

You will not be asked to learn to code. The person who builds it can sit elsewhere. The spend, the sequence, and the board conversation stay with you.
