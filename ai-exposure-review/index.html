Proposed entry

An AI exposure review, before a month of work.

People looking for an AI readiness audit are usually looking for a verdict they can use. $25,000 is a wide step from a free conversation. The proposed review is a smaller first meeting with a written result: what AI spend and activity already exist, which of it has a case, where your own gaps sit relative to the role you hold, and what a month of work would actually cover for you.

The shape in the brief is a 90-minute working session plus a short written assessment. That description is a proposal, not a scoped product.

What it is not

  • Not an audit we email for you to interpret alone. If a walkthrough is part of it, you book the conversation. You do not receive a file and a silence.
  • Not a tool score and not a vendor shortlist dressed up as advice.
  • Not the 30-day primer. The primer, if confirmed, is a separate month. The review would be credited toward it only if Colin says so.
  • Not the 15-minute roadmap. That session stays free and stays on its own page.

Until the offer exists, the honest next step is the roadmap, then a 20-minute readiness conversation about whether any paid work is the right fit.

Next step

Book the conversation. Do not ask for the review by email.
